## Anti-Slop & Design Engineering Standards (Gapless.id)
For any UI, visual design, copywriting, mobile responsiveness, or frontend code on Gapless:
- Strictly follow the Gapless Senior Frontend Design Engineering guide in [`SKILL.md`](file:///Users/nauvaldzakwanbaihaqi/Documents/gapless-v2/SKILL.md).
- Enforce the `antislop` plugin suite rules:
  - **UI / Visual (`antislop-ui`)**: Eliminate loud pastel pill-badges, generic AI gradients, and decorative sparkles (`✨`). Use clean neutral surfaces, hairline borders (`1px solid`), and direct visual diagrams over walls of text.
  - **Copywriting (`antislop-copywriting`)**: Direct, authentic, human-crafted Indonesian/English prose. Eliminate AI buzzwords ("Unlock your true potential", "Seamlessly navigate", etc.).
  - **Mobile Layout (`antislop-layoutmobile`)**: Mobile-first ergonomics, safe tap targets (`min-h-[44px]`), no horizontal overflow, and responsive data reflow.
  - **Human & Accessibility (`antislop-human`)**: High contrast, tactile button feedback (`active:scale-[0.97]`), and proper focus states.
  - **Code Hygiene (`antislop-code`)**: Clean code, zero unnecessary boilerplate, tabular-nums on all metrics, and zero business logic mutation.
